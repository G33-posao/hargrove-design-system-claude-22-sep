**Eyebrow** — the small uppercase kicker that sits above a headline; a core Hargrove pattern.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Why Hargrove?</Eyebrow>
<h2>Built on four commitments</h2>

<Eyebrow onNavy>The Hargrove Platform</Eyebrow>
```

Always sentence/section-case content, rendered uppercase by CSS. Muted grey on light; `onNavy` switches to the cool tint. Never used as a standalone heading — always paired with a headline beneath.
