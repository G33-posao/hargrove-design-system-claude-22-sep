**IconChip** — navy rounded-square tile with a white outline icon; the recurring "chip on a card" motif.

```jsx
<IconChip><i data-lucide="shield-check"></i></IconChip>

<Card>
  <IconChip overlap><i data-lucide="eye"></i></IconChip>
  <h3>Visibility</h3>
</Card>
```

Feed it a Lucide (or any outline/Feather-weight) SVG. `overlap` pulls it up over a card's top edge. Always navy fill, white icon — do not use filled icons or other colors.
