**CheckItem** — a navy square with a white check before a line of copy; the checklist-document motif.

```jsx
<CheckItem align="top">
  The client understands why estate planning matters to their financial plan.
</CheckItem>
```

Use `align="top"` for multi-line items so the box sits at the first line. The check is a built-in white outline glyph on a navy square — the one place a hand-drawn SVG is intentional (it's the brand's checkmark-box mark, not decorative iconography).
