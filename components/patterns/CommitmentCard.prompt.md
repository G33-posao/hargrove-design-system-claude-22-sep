**CommitmentCard** — the Four Commitments / advisor value-prop card (icon chip + bold navy title + grey one-liner).

```jsx
<CommitmentCard icon={<i data-lucide="shield-check" />} title="Accountability">
  A licensed attorney stands behind every plan.
</CommitmentCard>
```

Canonical Four Commitments (reuse verbatim): Accountability · Visibility · Fixed Price · Nationwide Scale. `layout="stack"` puts the chip above the title. Built on `Card` + `IconChip`.
