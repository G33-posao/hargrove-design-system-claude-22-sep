**Testimonial** — a quote card with attribution; the "In Their Words" / "What Our Clients Are Saying" motif.

```jsx
<Testimonial
  quote="What I value about Hargrove is the fixed-cost structure, the in-house experience, and the transparency of the process."
  name="Bob Glass" title="Director & Senior Wealth Advisor" firm="Mariner Wealth Advisors" />
```

Round headshot optional (pass `avatar` URL). Quotes are used verbatim from source. Left-aligned copy. Built on `Card`.
