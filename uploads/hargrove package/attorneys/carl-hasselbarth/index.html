Carl Hasselbarth

Attorney, Of Counsel Licensed in: New York

Carl Hasselbarth is an attorney and tax preparer licensed in New York. He specializes in estate planning and administration, tax law, and real estate matters. Carl graduated with distinction with his Bachelors in Business Administration from what is now called the University of Michigan Ross School of Business in 2003. At that time, the school was ranked by U.S. News World and Report as the #1 undergraduate business school in the country.
