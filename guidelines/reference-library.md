# Reference library — produced Hargrove pieces

Real client-approved work, kept for structural and visual inspiration. Copy layout patterns from these; **never lift their copy** into a different piece.

**Files shipped with the design system live in `guidelines/reference/`** — open the PDF and look at the pages before designing a piece of the same kind. The Sep 2026 set below is the client's own InDesign output and is the current standard for how new content is generated.

## Sep 2026 client-produced set (`guidelines/reference/`) — read first for any new piece

All five are InDesign 21.5 exports the client finished themselves. They show exactly how the brand rules land on a page: navy sandwich, letterspaced eyebrows, sentence-case headlines, `Hargrove Firm · [Doc Name]` + zero-padded page number footer, Inter only, one accent.

### `Hargrove-Ideal-Client-Profile.pdf` — 6-page print guide, 612×792 (Letter, px = pt)
The model for **any advisor-facing guide or white paper**.
- Navy cover: ALL-CAPS two-line title (`IDEAL CLIENT / PROFILE`), one-line sentence-case subtitle. No page number.
- Content pages `01`–`04`: one intro paragraph pair (no heading), then **question-word section headings** — `Who: The Clients Most Likely to Complete a Plan`, `When: Timing Can Make a Difference`, `How: Behaviors that Drive Conversion` — each with a bold one-line sub-statement beneath.
- **Stat pair pulled out beneath the paragraph** that states it: `82%` / `83%` with a short fragment of the source sentence as the label. The paragraph stays whole above; the callout reinforces, never replaces.
- **Ranked profile list**: bold lead phrase + em dash + explanation (`Married with minor children — the highest-converting…`). Bold carries the hierarchy; no numerals, no cards.
- **Quoted examples as a stack of indented quotes** (the five referral notes). Plain quotes, no speech-bubble graphics.
- Footnote markers on their word; note at the foot of that page, small (`1 This data comes from internal analysis…`).
- Closing page `In Plain Terms` (short summary paragraph) → CTA paragraph naming Mo Zoubi + email → one-line-per-clause disclaimer at the foot. No page number on the last page.

### `Hargrove-CAPTRUST-Onboarding.pdf` — 14-slide partner deck, 1920×1080
The model for **any partner / onboarding / implementation deck**.
- Cover: letterspaced eyebrow `P A R T N E R  O N B O A R D I N G · I M P L E M E N T A T I O N`, headline `HARGROVE × CAPTRUST`, one-sentence purpose line, date · audience line.
- **Two-part letterspaced eyebrow on every content slide**: `O V E R V I E W · Y O U R  T E A M`, `T H E  P R O C E S S · R O L L O U T  P L A N`, `P H A S E  O N E` … then a **sentence-case headline ending in a full stop** (`A team that knows your firm.`, `Why we're partnering.`, `Four phases.`).
- Team slide: five people in a row — name, letterspaced caps title, one-line role description, email. Closing bold line beneath (`Named contacts, not a support queue.`).
- **Label / value two-column table** (`Why CAPTRUST chose Hargrove | Filling the gap…`) for goals and for `WHAT WE NEED | WHY WE NEED IT`.
- **Commit / Ask pairing**: `W H AT  W E  C O M M I T  T O` (three columns) above `W H AT  W E  A S K  O F  C A P T R U S T` (checklist).
- Four-phase row `P H A S E 1–4` with title + two-line description, then a `T O  C O N F I R M` block of open questions.
- Big stat slides: `72%` with a *Why this matters:* sentence; `Days` as a word-stat under `F I R S T  R E F E R R A L  W I N D O W`.
- `30 / 60 / 90` day check-in columns, each a short bullet list, closed by a `R E C U R R I N G  S Y N C` line.
- Footer: `Hargrove Firm Advisor Success · Partner Onboarding  02`. **Note:** the cover carries "Confidential — prepared for CAPTRUST" as part of the client's own copy; that does not license a Confidential label in footers.

### `Hargrove-Advisors-Guide-to-LLCs.pdf` — 23-slide educational deck, 1920×1080
The model for **any CE / educational webinar deck** with dense bullet content.
- Cover: `Presented by Hargrove` kicker, two-line Title Case title.
- **Left-column slide title, right-column content** on every slide — title in 2–5 short lines (`Why Advisors / Should Care / About LLCs`), content as bullets or a small labelled sub-heading (`Legal Structure`, `Liability`, `Taxation`). The same slide title repeats across consecutive slides (`What is an LLC?` ×3) with a different sub-heading — that is how a topic is paced.
- Nested bullets keep the source's levels (see `deck-text-hierarchy.md`).
- **Quote-list slide**: client phrases in quotes as the bullets (`"I bought a rental property."`), followed by one plain closing sentence.
- **Follow-up framework**: bold question + indented sub-questions; a stat line (`~90% of clients never created an Operating Agreement`) sits inline.
- **Two-column contrast** slides: `LLC | Insurance`, `Appropriate Advisor Conversation | Things to Send to Counsel`, closed by a bold `Simple rule:` / `Result:`-style line.
- **Twelve-item grid** of short phrases (`Common LLC Mistakes We See`) — three-to-five-word cells, no icons.
- **Pricing slides one tier per slide**: big price left (`$1,250`), `Tier 1 – Single-Member Entity Document Package` title, `For clients…` line, `Includes:` line, compliance-checklist line. `Custom Pricing` as the word-price for Tier 4.
- Footer `Hargrove Firm · The Advisor's Guide to LLCs  02`.

### `Hargrove-Estate-Planning-Playbook.pdf` — 16-slide webinar deck, 1920×1080
The model for **the Estate Planning Playbook webinar series** and any short talk deck.
- Cover: letterspaced series eyebrow `E S T A T E  P L A N N I N G  P L A Y B O O K  W E B I N A R  S E R I E S`, ALL-CAPS three-line title (`IRREVOCABLE TRUST / — IS IT REALLY THAT / IRREVOCABLE?`), `Presented by …`, date | time.
- `Meet the Panelists` slide `02`: name / title / entity (Hargrove Firm vs Hargrove MSO — keep the entity line).
- **Navy statement dividers** (no page number): ALL-CAPS two-line title + one sentence-case line or a quoted question (`"Can we just make a few changes to this Irrevocable trust?"`). Four of them pace the deck.
- Content slides: Title Case headline, four to six single-level bullets. **Three named roles** as three columns (`Advisory Committee | Trust Protector | Independent Special Trustee`) with a shared sentence above and a 2×2 of outcomes below.
- `Closing Takeaway` slide → `Next Steps` (two labelled contacts: `Email questions:` / `Schedule a demo:`) → `Q&A` → `Thank you`, the last two centred single words on navy, unnumbered.
- Footer `Hargrove Firm · Estate Planning Playbook  02`.

### `Hargrove-Mariner-Speakers-Card.pdf` — 1200×1200 LinkedIn card
The model for **event / speaker social cards**.
- ALL-CAPS three-line announcement (`WE'RE SPEAKING AT / MARINER'S / CONFERENCE!`), date range | city line.
- **Three speakers in a row**: round headshot, name, title in two lines where needed (`Advanced Planning / Attorney`). Uses the Jessi Konrad / Brittany Marks / Mo Zoubi headshots in `assets/people/`.
- Complements `social-linkedin.md`; when the two differ, the card (client-produced, Sep 2026) is the later word.

### What these five settle
- **Eyebrow register for decks**: letterspaced caps, often two parts joined by `·`. Sentence-case headline beneath, full stop allowed on partner decks.
- **Headline register**: ALL CAPS on covers and navy dividers; Title Case or sentence case on content slides, following the source.
- **Stats**: pulled out *beneath* or *beside* the sentence that states them, labelled with a fragment of that sentence.
- **Lists**: bold lead + em dash + explanation for ranked items; plain quotes for example phrases; nested levels preserved.
- **Contacts**: labelled (`Email questions:` / `Schedule a demo:`), named person + `@hargrovemso.com`.
- Every content page/slide carries the invariant footer; covers, dividers, Q&A and Thank-you pages do not.

## Advanced Estate Planning in Practice (webinar deck, 21 slides)
`uploads/Advanced Estate Planning Webinar v2.pdf` — the fullest worked example of a Hargrove deck.

**Structure**
1. Navy cover — letterspaced eyebrow `ADVISOR WEBINAR SERIES`, ALL-CAPS three-line title, subtitle, then date / time / platform / "Presented by Hargrove Firm" on one line.
2. **Meet the Panelists** — round headshots, name / title / entity stacked beneath.
3. Navy section divider — ALL-CAPS two-line title + one-line subtitle.
4–7. Light content slides.
8. Navy section divider.
9–20. Light content slides.
21. Closing.

**Patterns worth reusing**
- **Eyebrow as widely letterspaced caps** — `S T A T E   E X P O S U R E   ≠   F E D E R A L   E X P O S U R E`, `A N T I - C L A W B A C K   P R O T E C T I O N`, `C H A L L E N G E`, `S O L U T I O N`. Sits above a sentence-case headline.
- **`NOT` / `INSTEAD` two-column reframe** — a wrong question against the right one.
- **Stat pulled out beside the prose** — `$15M / $30M`, `+5%` with a short qualifying line beneath.
- **Labelled step columns** for a strategy walkthrough (Create a Family LLC → Divide Ownership → Transfer Non-Voting Interests), closed by a bold `Result:` line.
- **`Key Question:` / `Key Point:` / `Result:`** as inline bold leads inside a slide.
- Footer on content slides: `Hargrove Firm · Advanced Estate Planning in Practice` — **no "Confidential"**, correctly.

**What NOT to copy from it**
- Its **pagination varies** — sizes and treatments shift between slides. That was the old approach. Pagination is now one fixed treatment: zero-padded `01`, `02`, `03`, same size and position on every content page, never designed. See `pagination.md`.
