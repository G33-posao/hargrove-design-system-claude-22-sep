# 2026 Brand Guideline Deck — the primary brand reference

Source: `uploads/Hargrove-2026-Brand-Guideline-Deck.pdf` (17 pages, client-approved, Aug 2026). **This is the most authoritative brand document in the system** — when anything elsewhere conflicts with it, this deck wins. Structure: Brand Foundation → Messaging & Communication → Visual Identity.

## Brand Foundation

**Mission Statement (verbatim):** Hargrove is purpose-built for the financial services industry to bridge the gap between financial advisors, their clients, and estate planning attorneys. Our mission is to make estate planning accessible, seamless, and integrated into the broader financial advisory process, ensuring clients' legacies are protected.

**Unique Value Proposition (verbatim):** Hargrove Firm is the only law firm that provides high-quality estate planning services combining the accountability of a top-tier law firm with the efficiency and visibility of a premium tech platform — at a fixed price on a national scale.

**Brand Pillars (canonical wording):**
| Pillar | Line |
| --- | --- |
| ACCOUNTABILITY | Top-tier law firm standing behind every engagement |
| VISIBILITY | Process visibility powered by a premium tech platform |
| FIXED PRICE | Predictable, transparent fees — no surprises |
| NATIONAL SCOPE | An existing team of attorneys across all 50 states |

> Note: this deck names the fourth pillar **NATIONAL SCOPE** (the older Four Commitments material said "Nationwide Scale"; the website segment cards say "Scalability" / "National Coverage" per audience). In brand-level material, follow the deck.

**Target Audience:** Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs); wealth planning decision-makers at financial firms; financial advisors and wealth managers; directors or heads of planning departments; financial professionals focused on high-net-worth or mass affluent clients. Ideal clients are advisors seeking a reliable, integrated estate planning solution who want to maintain their role in their clients' financial lives.

## Messaging & Communication

**Brand Voice:** Confident, clear, credible, and human — direct, measured, and grounded in legal authority.
- **Confident** — Not arrogant. We say what we mean and stand behind it.
- **Clear** — No jargon, no hedging. Plain language that respects busy professionals.
- **Credible** — Grounded in legal authority and professional accountability.
- **Human** — Warm where it counts. Estate planning is personal—we don't forget that.

Be declarative, precise, and transparent. Be accessible and steady — write for a smart non-lawyer with a calm, reassuring tone.

**Taglines (verbatim):**
- Primary: **The Estate Plan You Deserve. The Accountability You Need.**
- Descriptive: **Helping RIAs deliver attorney-led estate planning at national scale**

**Writing style — preferred vs. avoid (the deck's full table):**
| Preferred | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed price / one price | Competitive pricing / affordable |
| Accountability | Peace of mind |
| Visibility / track in real time | Seamless / integrated |
| Licensed attorneys | Legal professionals / our team |
| Nationwide / nationally scalable | Anywhere / everywhere |
| Estate plan / plan | Product / solution / offering |
| Documents included | Comprehensive / full-service |
| Know what it costs | No hidden fees / transparent pricing |

Also avoid: *affordable, seamless, comprehensive, no hidden fees* as generic marketing phrases. Every sentence should reflect the voice of a modern law firm powered by technology.

> ⚠️ One nuance: the deck's own **mission statement** contains "seamless" and "integrated" — that text is client-approved and reproduced verbatim; the avoid-list governs copy **we** write.

## Visual Identity

**Typography:** Inter (rsms.me/inter), weights 300–900 shown. Confirms Inter-only; no Inter Display, no second family.

**Color palette — the deck's official split:**
- **Primary:** Navy `#16335D` (RGB 22,51,93 · CMYK 100,82,36,27) and Black `#000000`.
- **Secondary:** `#F8F8F8` · `#9FC2F0` · `#696969` · `#999999`.

> `#9FC2F0` is hereby an official secondary (previously tracked as a derived on-navy link tint). Still: navy is the one accent; `#9FC2F0` is a supporting tint on navy surfaces, never a competing accent.

**Logo vs. tagline (usage rule):**
- **Hargrove logo alone** — our materials and web, where we control the copy.
- **Logo with the "attorney-led planning" tagline lockup** — outside our materials (other websites, conferences, sponsorships…), where it helps explain what we do.
