## Headshots

Variants live in `../assets/people/`:

- **`assets/people/*.jpg` — the ORIGINALS (colour). Use these by default**, in every normal case (round crops in decks, brochures, cards, the People card). Square headshots on a plain background.
- **`assets/people/bw/*.jpg` — black-and-white original.** Use when the piece follows the live site's B&W portrait treatment (attorney roster, team pages), or when several people sit together and only some photos are colour — B&W unifies them.
- **`assets/people/cutout/*.png` — background removed (transparent, colour).** Use ONLY when the design genuinely needs the person to sit on a coloured or navy field with no visible edge — e.g. a figure bleeding off a navy cover, or a cut-out over a tone-on-tone panel. Not for round crops; a round crop should always use an original.
- **`assets/people/cutout-bw/*.png` — transparent AND black-and-white.** The cut-out case above, when the piece is B&W.
